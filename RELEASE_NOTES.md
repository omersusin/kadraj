# Kadraj v1.2.0

An editorial collage studio for screenshots and photos, now with a warmer, more tactile interface and a deeper photo-finishing toolkit.

## Highlights

- **A new visual language** — a slow-drifting aurora backdrop, warm glass surfaces, gradient lighting, and softly floating controls.
- **Tone adjustments** — light, contrast, color, and warmth sliders for every photo, with live preview.
- **Auto Enhance** — one tap adds gentle film color, light, contrast, and warmth across the whole set.
- **Sync to All** — carry the selected photo's look and tone through every frame.
- **Composition guides** — a rule-of-thirds overlay with a center mark, toggled beside the canvas ratio.
- **Caption ideas** — quick starter lines when the caption is empty, plus a one-tap clear action.
- **Two new palettes** — Sunset and Lagoon join the collection, for ten in total.
- **More motion** — animated tab transitions, springy palette selection, glowing sliders, and a living stage backdrop.

## Everything else

- Smart layout engine that reads image orientation and ratio variety.
- Auto, Grid, Flow, Hero, Strip, and Stack layouts.
- Ten palettes: Paper, Night, Clay, Sage, Mist, Rose, Ivory, Olive, Sunset, and Lagoon.
- Per-photo looks with adjustable strength; zoom, tilt, flips, duplicate, and reset.
- Captions with serif/sans, size, placement, alignment, tone, and plate controls.
- Spacing, margin, corner, mat, shadow, and grain controls, plus Surprise Me and Reset Style.
- Undo and redo up to 40 steps.
- Auto, 2K, and 4K export in JPEG or PNG; saved to the `Kadraj` album in Photos.
- No internet permission, no account, no cloud. Everything runs on-device.

## Install

1. Download `kadraj-v1.2.0.apk`.
2. Allow app installs from this source in Android settings.
3. Open the APK and install.

Android 10 and up is required. The package is `com.omersusin.kadraj`; this and future releases are signed with the same release key.
