# Kadraj v2.0.0

A deeper studio, with the same quiet surface. This release turns the editor into a more complete composing tool without giving up its on-device, photo-first character.

## Highlights

- **Composition presets** — Studio, Gallery, Zine, Noir, Postcard, and Bento combine a layout, ratio, palette, spacing, material, and grain in one tap. Your caption is preserved.
- **Smart enhance** — each photo is sampled on-device and balanced for light, contrast, colour, and warmth instead of receiving one fixed filter.
- **Smart crop** — a light edge-and-chroma analysis finds the visual centre of a photo and moves the crop there.
- **Finish controls** — add fade, vignette, and per-photo grain; rotate a frame in 90° steps.
- **Original preview** — the eye on the stage shows the untouched photo treatment, including the reset crop, so you can judge the edit in context.
- **Cancellable export** — long 4K renders can be stopped from the export sheet without leaving the editor.

## Themes

- **Cinema** is the default: dark stage, peach light, floating controls.
- **Paper** is a warm editorial skin with Fraunces headlines, terracotta accents, square frames, and a paper matte.
- **Contact** is a monochrome contact-sheet skin with monospace labels, hairline borders, a technical grid, and signal red.

Switch at any time from **Settings** (the gear on the start screen or in the editor). The choice survives restarts.

## Everything else

- Smart layout engine that reads image orientation and ratio variety.
- Auto, Grid, Flow, Hero, Strip, and Stack layouts.
- Ten palettes: Paper, Night, Clay, Sage, Mist, Rose, Ivory, Olive, Sunset, and Lagoon.
- Per-photo looks with adjustable strength; light, contrast, colour, and warmth; zoom, tilt, flips, duplicate, and reset.
- Captions with serif/sans, size, placement, alignment, tone, and plate controls.
- Spacing, margin, corner, mat, shadow, and grain controls, plus Surprise Me and Reset Style.
- Undo and redo up to 40 steps.
- Up to 30 photos in one collage.
- Auto, 2K, and 4K export in JPEG or PNG; saved to the `Kadraj` album in Photos.
- No internet permission, no account, no cloud. Everything runs on-device.

## Install

1. Download `kadraj-v2.0.0.apk`.
2. Allow app installs from this source in Android settings.
3. Open the APK and install.

Android 10 and up is required. The package is `com.omersusin.kadraj`; this and future releases are signed with the same release key.
